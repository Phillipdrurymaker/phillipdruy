My story

I fix lists that everyone forgot to follow up with.

How many subscribers have opted in this month and heard nothing back? How many emails go out to your entire list every time, because there was never a system built to speak to people differently based on what they actually want?

I studied marketing at the University of Ilorin, and since then I've worked across email marketing, Shopify and WordPress builds, and automation with n8n — because an email system doesn't live on its own. It has to connect to the store, the CRM, the actual buying journey.

That's the gap I close. Not a template dropped into your account — a system built around your list, tested before it ever reaches a subscriber, and handed over with you knowing exactly how it works.
