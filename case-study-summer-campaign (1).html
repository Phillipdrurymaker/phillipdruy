Summer Sale Campaign Series

A 3-part Klaviyo email sequence built around a real on-site summer promo, its shipping-cutoff urgency, and two featured product bundles.

The project

The store was running a summer promo on-site — "Train Hard This Summer, We'll Handle The Shipping" — built around a same-day shipping cutoff (order before 4pm PST) and trust signals like 2,000+ reviews and USA-made, lab-tested products.

None of that messaging was reaching the email list. Subscribers who'd already bought from the store had no idea the promo existed unless they happened to visit the site directly.

Reference: the store's on-site summer promo banner
Reference: the two bundles featured in the campaign

What I built

Three emails, each with a distinct job — segmented by purchase history so each subscriber saw a bundle relevant to what they'd already bought.

01
Teaser email

Introduced the same-day shipping cutoff as the campaign's core urgency hook — sent 2 days before the promo went live.

02
Launch email

Spotlighted the Cycle Defender + PCT bundle and the Advanced Fat Loss + Mood Enhancement stack, with the trust bar (2,000+ reviews, lab-tested, USA-made) repeated as social proof.

03
Last-call email

Re-sent only to non-openers, with the 4pm PST cutoff reframed as the closing line — last chance for same-day shipping before the promo ends.

04
Segmentation

Split by past purchase category — pre-workout buyers vs. recovery/PCT buyers — so the bundle shown matched what each subscriber already used.

Tools & approach

Klaviyo flows & campaigns List segmentation Urgency copywriting Mobile rendering checks Send-time testing
