Welcome Series Flow Rebuild

A single flat welcome email rebuilt into a 3-part Klaviyo flow with branching logic based on purchase status.

The project

New subscribers to the brand's list got exactly one email: a welcome message with a discount code. No follow-up, no education on what makes the brand worth trusting, and no distinction between someone who'd already bought and someone still deciding.

Anyone who converted off the discount kept getting the same welcome messaging anyway — because the flow had no way to check.

The rebuilt flow — trigger, 3 emails, and a purchase-status split
Reference: brand visual identity used across the flow

What I built

Three emails plus a conditional split — so the flow stops treating buyers and non-buyers the same way.

01
Email 1 — Welcome + offer

Delivers a 10% first-order code, timed for the critical first-hour open window right after signup.

02
Email 2 — Trust, not another discount

Sent a day later. Shifts from the offer to the brand's clean-ingredient promise and a simple skincare routine — building the case for people who haven't bought yet.

03
Purchase-status split

Before the final email, the flow checks: did this subscriber already buy? If yes, they exit into a separate post-purchase flow instead of getting re-pitched the same welcome offer.

04
Email 3 — Social proof

Sent only to subscribers who haven't converted yet, two days on — bestsellers, ratings, and real customer photos, no discount code this time.

Tools & approach

Klaviyo flow builder Conditional splits Purchase-based segmentation Timing & send-window strategy Social proof copywriting

"A welcome series shouldn't keep selling the same discount to someone who already bought. The split is the whole point — it's what turns a generic sequence into one that actually pays attention."

— Dammy, on this project
