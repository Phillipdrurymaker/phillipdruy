KLAVIYO · SUPPLEMENTS / E-COMMERCE

Summer sale campaign series

A sports nutrition store was running a summer promo on-site — "Train Hard This Summer, We'll Handle The Shipping," built around a same-day shipping cutoff (order before 4pm PST) and trust signals like 2,000+ reviews and USA-made, lab-tested products. None of that was reaching the email list.

The build: a 3-part Klaviyo campaign turning the on-site promo into a sending sequence — a teaser email introducing the shipping-cutoff urgency, a launch email spotlighting two real bundles (Cycle Defender + PCT, and the Advanced Fat Loss + Mood Enhancement stack) with the trust bar repeated as social proof, and a last-call email re-sent only to non-openers with the 4pm cutoff as the closing line. Segmented by past purchase category (pre-workout vs. recovery) so each subscriber saw a bundle relevant to what they'd already bought.

Sample project — built to show process, not tied to a live client yet.
